🆘 Joggin V1.70 — Safety alert

• New in Options: Safety alert. If you stop moving for 3 minutes during an activity, Joggin asks "Are you OK?". If you don't answer within 1 minute, it texts your chosen contact with a map link to your location
• Pick your contact from your phone's contacts and send a test text to check it works
• Tap "I'm OK" or start moving again to cancel; pausing turns it off
• The arrows that open and close sections in Options are bigger and easier to see
