🤝 Joggin V1.71 — Walk together

• New: invite a buddy to walk, run or ride together. If they accept, the activity starts on both phones at once
• Invite from the home screen, or tap "Invite to join" next to a buddy in Buddy Link. If several buddies are online, you pick who to invite
• During the activity your buddy's route shows on your map as a blue dotted line, beside your own where you walk the same way
• When you finish, your buddy's route is saved with your activity and shows on the summary and in your history
• Your route is shared with your buddy for that activity only. Their phone keeps a copy of it with that activity in their history
• New in Options: show or hide the exercise rings on the home screen
• Buddy locations update straight away when your connection comes back
